# Research notes

The raw research behind [the study](../README.md): fourteen notes written while researching procedural pixel art and worlds for Tycho, between 2026-11-02 and 2026-11-04. The study's pages rewrite them into the docs' style; the notes keep everything, with each claim's source inline and the line drawn between cited findings, inferences and gaps. They're kept as written, so some of their statements were later corrected by measurement (the Diablo II sheet study corrects the style note's "dark rim") or by the pages. Every source the pages cite is also in [Sources](../sources.md).
